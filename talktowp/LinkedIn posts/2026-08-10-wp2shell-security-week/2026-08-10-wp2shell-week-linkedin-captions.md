## Day 3 — Wed Aug 12, 6:30pm IST — "The hidden backdoor" (peak engagement day)
File: day3-mu-plugins.pdf (7 slides)

**Caption:**
There's a WordPress folder that never shows up on your Plugins screen. Researchers found a backdoor campaign hiding inside it this month.

Must use plugins in /wp-content/mu-plugins/ load automatically in the background. No toggle, no listing, nothing in wp-admin ever points you there. That makes it a genuinely good place for something to hide.

A site could be compromised right now with the owner having zero reason to ever check, because there's never been a reason to. Swipe through for how to look, and what to do if something looks off.

TalktoWP watches for unexpected file changes across your whole site fleet, not just what wp-admin chooses to show you. Free for your first site.

File: day4-93bugs.pdf (7 slides)

**Caption:**
wp2shell got the headlines this July. Ninety three other WordPress vulnerabilities shipped patches the same week, across core, plugins, and themes.

Yoast SEO and BuddyPress were both on that list. Not obscure tools nobody's heard of. Most sites run a dozen or more plugins, so the odds that at least one has an update sitting there unapplied are not small.

Now multiply that across every client site an agency manages. Dozens of sites, each with a dozen plus plugins, each needing a human to notice a patch shipped. That does not scale, and it's exactly how most WordPress sites actually get compromised.
